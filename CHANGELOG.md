## [v1.0.3] – 2025-11-26  
### Added  
- Added **PDF versions** of all Markdown documents located in the `/papers` directory.  
  These include the Declaration of Creation, Framework, Prototype documentation,  
  The Rodić Principle, Mathematical Appendix, and all related research texts.  
  PDFs provide publication-ready formatting and long-term archival stability.

### Purpose  
This release strengthens the **documentation layer** of the project.  
No code or structural logic was modified.  
The update ensures compatibility with academic citation standards,  
offline reading, and external distribution outside GitHub environments.

## [Initial Release] – 2025-11-05  

### Added  
- Established the **dual-licensed open framework** combining  
  **Conscience by Design (framework)** and **Conscience Layer Prototype (ethical AI core)**.  

- Implemented foundational repository structure:
  - `LICENSE.md` (CC BY 4.0 + MIT dual license)  
  - `README.md`, `CONTRIBUTING.md`, `ACKNOWLEDGMENTS.md`, `AUTHORS.md`, `.gitignore`  
  - `MANIFEST_OF_DONATION.md` – Declaring the project as a public ethical contribution to humanity.  
  - `papers/` – All primary research, philosophical foundations, architectural models,  
    and the Declaration of Creation.  
  - `prototype/conscience_layer_prototype.py` – First working module implementing  
    the SRQ model, TIS/HAI/SRQ pipeline, and the ethical audit log.  

### Notes  
This release marks the **official inception of the Conscience by Design initiative (2025)** –  
a research and development framework authored by **Aleksandar Rodić**,  
dedicated to embedding *ethical awareness, moral reasoning, and human alignment*  
into artificial intelligence.
